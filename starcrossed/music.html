Our Playlists

for my summer bee

Kelsie made this playlist for me on June 5, 2026. 3 weeks into summer long distance, we had been arguing a lot and after our last fight, Kelsie spent hours curating a playlist with a very specific order and a lot of her favorite songs relating to long distance love. She wanted us to reconnect through the playlist. It made me so emotional and we felt very connected. I found some amazing songs through it and rated each one as we listened together. - Rohit

starcrossed

We made this playlist during our date on September 13, 2025. The date was in St. Louis, we were going on a mini road trip to the nearest Dutch Bros because Kelsie wanted to show me Dutch Bros. On the way, we wanted to make a playlist of all of "our songs". Kelsie was nervous to tell me the name she came up with for it: starcrossed, but I loved it and was confused why she was nervous. We added just 12 songs then, and have added songs to it as we discover them and as they develop into an "our song". - Rohit
